Detector Details & Limitations:
1. DDoS Floods:
   - Evaluated by DDoSVolumetricRule (pps > 250, syn_ratio > 0.80) and Random Forest.
   - Tested in test_ddos_rule and verify_system.py.
   - Normal traffic does NOT trigger false positives (tested in test_pipeline.py).
   - Limitation: Slow rate-distributed DDoS below 250 pkts/s relies on Isolation Forest anomaly detection.

2. Botnet C2 Beaconing:
   - Evaluated by BotnetBeaconRule requiring >= 4 observations with interval jitter < 0.20s, plus Random Forest.
   - Tested in test_botnet_normal_https_does_not_trigger and test_botnet_synthetic_beacon_sequence_triggers.
   - Normal HTTPS does NOT trigger false positives (proven by unit test).
   - Limitation: Extremely irregular C2 channels with high artificial jitter (> 0.5s) require multi-day observation windows.

3. DGA / DNS Tunneling:
   - Evaluated by DNSTunnelingRule (Shannon entropy > 3.5 bits, length > 28) and Random Forest.
   - Tested in test_dns_tunneling_rule and verify_system.py.
   - Normal domains (google.com, microsoft.com) do NOT trigger false positives.
   - Limitation: CDN domains with random hash prefixes may trigger low-confidence alerts; mitigated by local threat intel whitelist.

4. Suspicious Encrypted Traffic:
   - Evaluated by EncryptedAnomalyRule (TLS on non-standard ports or anomalous bursts) and Random Forest.
   - Tested in test_ml_inference_and_anomaly and verify_system.py.
   - Standard HTTPS on port 443 with normal baseline does NOT trigger false positives.
   - Limitation: Does NOT inspect encrypted payloads (by strict unidirectional compliance design).

5. Port Scanning & Reconnaissance:
   - Evaluated by PortScanRule (>= 15 ports or >= 10 destination hosts) and Random Forest.
   - Tested in test_port_scan_rule and verify_system.py.
   - Normal multi-tab browsing does NOT trigger false positives due to fan-out thresholds.
   - Limitation: In unidirectional traffic, TCP SYN packets are visible but server RSTs are absent; scan success is inferred from subsequent sessions.

6. Data Exfiltration:
   - Evaluated by DataExfiltrationRule (bytes > 4MB with asymmetric ratio > 10.0) and Random Forest.
   - Tested in test_data_exfiltration_rule and verify_system.py.
   - Normal inbound downloads do NOT trigger outbound exfiltration alerts.
   - Limitation: Legitimate bulk backups outward may trigger alerts unless whitelisted in local baseline.
